# Fylvix — Worldwide Entertainment Streaming & Discovery Platform

**Fylvix** is a complete, scalable, production-ready worldwide entertainment streaming and discovery platform engineered with a modern dark cinematic UI, responsive multi-device layouts (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, and Large Displays), clean layered architecture, PostgreSQL-compatible data models, signed CDN/HLS playback delivery, configurable 18+ content classification controls, and a full built-in Admin Dashboard.

## Core Capabilities

- **Global Content Catalog**: Worldwide Movies, TV Shows, Series (Show → Season → Episode hierarchy), Anime (Subbed/Dubbed, Ongoing/Completed, Characters), Documentaries, Cartoons, Kids & Family, Hollywood, Bollywood, Pakistani, Turkish, Korean, Japanese, Arabic, European, African, and International cinema.
- **Configurable 18+ / Adult Section**: Backend- and Admin-controlled content classification (`18+`), regional availability, homepage visibility, and search visibility—without intrusive repetitive age-confirmation popups on every visit.
- **Live TV & EPG**: Authorized Live TV channels across News, Sports, Entertainment, Movies, Music, Kids, Documentary, and International categories with real-time current/next program schedules and live stream playback.
- **Global Search & Discovery**: Instant suggestions, debounced search, search history, and multi-faceted filtering by Type, Genre, Country, Language, Release Year, Rating, Runtime, Certification, and Video Quality.
- **Professional Streaming Player**: Interactive HLS/DASH-ready player with Play/Pause, Seek, Volume/Mute, Fullscreen, Picture-in-Picture, Quality Selector (`Auto`, `4K UHD`, `1080p`, `720p`, `480p`), Subtitle Track Selector, Audio Language Selector, Playback Speed (`0.5x` – `2.0x`), Skip Intro, Episode Drawer, Next/Previous Episode, and automatic Resume/Continue Watching progress sync.
- **User System & Multi-Profile Architecture**: Registration, Login, Forgot/Reset Password, Multiple Profiles (Main, Cinema Fan, Kids, etc.), My List, Favorites, Watch History, Ratings, and Personalized Recommendations.
- **Full Admin Panel**: Manage Movies, Series/Seasons/Episodes, Anime, 18+ Content & Policy Controls, Live TV Channels, Genres/Countries/Languages, Homepage Hero Banners, Metadata Provider Adapters, Analytics, and System Audit Logs.
